Why it exists

Built to rest a hand on the keys

I have a physical disability. To reach something on the far side of my desk, I often have to rest my hand or arm on the keyboard, and it types whatever is under it.

Keyless lets me lean on the keyboard and keep using the mouse.

How it works

Small, and stays out of the way

  • Ctrl + Alt + K anywhere, or the switch in the tray
  • A small tag shows while paused; click Resume to turn keys back on
  • Auto-resume after 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes
  • Resumes by itself when you lock the screen, sign out or sleep
  • 10 languages, light and dark

Nothing leaves your PC

Keyless never records, stores or sends keystrokes, and it makes no network connections. The code is open, so you can check.
